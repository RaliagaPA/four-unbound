FOUR UNBOUND
An expanding Connect Four experiment · gravity stays on

How to play: Click any column arrow or cell to drop a piece. Connect four to win. Pieces fall with gravity, and full columns expand upward. Side expansion unlocks when the bottom row has 0, 1, or 2 empty spaces left in 2-, 3-, or 4-player games, respectively.

Controls: Changing players, board size, or turn order restarts the game. Undo rewinds your last round.
